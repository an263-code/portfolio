**Product Designer** — Alida *(2022.05–20XX)*

Alida is a B2B SaaS platform that helps enterprise brands build and manage research communities, giving insights, UX, and market research teams a single place to run surveys, interviews, and other research studies against a persistent, opted-in audience. Serving global enterprise customers including LinkedIn, Warner Bros. Discovery, the Financial Times, and Red Bull.

As the sole designer on the insights and reporting side of the platform, I owned the end-to-end redesign of the survey reporting system and the dashboard product, which is the core surfaces where researchers and non-researcher stakeholders analyze and share findings. 

**Reporting System Migration**

- Led the full migration of the survey reporting system beyond feature parity — reaching **30% customer adoption within the first 2 months** of launch and **60% within 24 months**
- Migrated users spend about **45 min per session** on average, more than double the **20 min** on the legacy system — reflecting meaningfully deeper engagement with the redesigned experience
- Introduced AI-powered capabilities within 3 months of launch: AI Summary (**35%** user adoption), AI Assistant Chatbot (**25%**), Sentiment Analysis and Smart Grouping (**10%** each) 
- Mastered complex statistical methods (Crosstabs, Conjoint, MaxDiff, TURF) from scratch through expert feedback sessions, then simplified their configuration flows for professional researchers 

**UX Research Analysis**

- Designed the reporting layer for the platform's first dedicated UX research analysis tools, including prototype testing, card sorting, and tree testing, extending the platform into a full mixed-method analysis surface 
- Built a video analysis system supporting per-recording review, cross-video insight synthesis, clip creation, and showreel production

**Data Quality**

- Designed a data cleaning system embedded directly in the report: an Excel-like response table with manual tagging and AI-assisted automatic flagging of low-quality responses

**Dashboard Revamp**

- Redesigned the dashboard product to support multiple data sources built on the new reporting architecture, streamlining how researchers build and share findings with non-researcher stakeholders
